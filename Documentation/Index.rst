============
Page Archive
============

:Extension key:
    page_archive

:Package name:
    knowex/page-archive

:Version:
    |release|

:Rendered:
    |today|

----

Old pages clutter the backend page tree, but editors often do not want to delete
them because the content may be needed again. This extension archives such
pages: they disappear from the page tree and from the website, stay in the
database unchanged and can be restored with one click.
